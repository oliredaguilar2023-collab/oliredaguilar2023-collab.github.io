Professional process

From messy requirement to working system.

A straightforward process designed to keep the build understandable, testable and documented.

01

Discover

Understand the business, lead sources, sales process, tools, bottlenecks and desired outcome.

02

Map

Turn the requirement into a clear process: lead capture → CRM → qualification → appointment → follow-up.

03

Build

Configure the CRM, pipeline, forms, workflows, calendars, integrations and automation logic.

04

Test

Test triggers, actions, routing, messages, webhooks and edge cases before launch.

05

Document

Provide practical documentation so the system is easier to understand, maintain and troubleshoot.

06

Improve

Clean up, optimize and support the system as business requirements evolve.
